Shorts Pipeline

Part of My Clips Project

Shorts Pipeline is a personal, self-hosted tool. It runs on its operator's own Windows PC, cuts highlights from livestreams the operator is permitted to clip, adds subtitles, and uploads the finished vertical videos to the operator's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

Who it is for: only the operator of this project and their own YouTube channel. It is not a public service and does not offer accounts to other people.

What it does with your Google account: uploads videos you created with the tool, reads your channel name to confirm the right channel is connected, and checks the visibility of the videos it uploaded. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
